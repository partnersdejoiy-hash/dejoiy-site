DEJOIY | Prepare an authorised employment check.
Updated: 29 September 2026

Understand what to include, what to leave out and how the review works before submitting.

IDENTIFY THE REQUEST
- Provide the requesting organisation, a work email and the employee’s full name.
- Include an employee ID if available, and describe the purpose and exact scope of the check.
- Only request information you have permission to obtain.

PREPARE THE AUTHORISATION
- Attach the employee authorisation letter as a PDF no larger than 2 MB.
- Make sure the authorisation covers your organisation and the intended verification.
- Do not attach government IDs, bank details or unrelated personal records.

UNDERSTAND THE REVIEW
- HR checks the underlying employment record and requester authority before sharing information.
- Access to an email inbox does not, by itself, prove authority to receive employment information.
- The team may ask for clarification; turnaround depends on the records and checks needed.

KEEP THE REFERENCE
- Save the submission reference and quote it in follow-ups.
- Email-only submissions are followed up by email. Private portal requests have online history when the portal is available.
- Keep private access links to yourself and download only documents intended for you.
